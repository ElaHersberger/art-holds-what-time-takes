## 5. Terminology Governance

The repository distinguishes between three types of terminology.

### Established

Concepts and language already adopted as part of Ela's artistic identity or Masterpiece Artwork's brand.

Examples:

- Art Holds What Time Takes
- My Process
- ART REMAINS. — Masterpiece Artwork

### Emerging

Concepts actively being developed for Ela's professional specialization.

Examples:

- Personality Before Palette
- Harmony Without Uniformity
- The Person · The Art · The Space
- The Wall Art Consultation

### Proposed

Possible future brand language that has not been formally adopted.

Examples:

- The Artist Who Understands the Wall
- The Art of Belonging
- Connection Before Decoration

Proposed terminology must not be presented as an established proprietary framework, registered trademark, or official brand signature without founder confirmation and appropriate supporting evidence.
