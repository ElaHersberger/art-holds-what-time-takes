## Featured Documentation

### Fine Art in Interiors — Visual Placement Studies

[Explore the Fine Art Interior Placement Portfolio](fine-art-interior-placement-studies.md)

Four visual studies demonstrate how artwork can influence the atmosphere, character, color harmony, and visual composition of residential interiors.

The portfolio explores:

1. **Nostalgia & Atmosphere** — Christmas fine art in a traditional interior.
2. **Coastal Romance** — Fine art as the focal point of a residential space.
3. **Personal Storytelling** — Artwork, personal identity, and interior harmony.
4. **Architectural Scale** — Artwork placement within a large architectural environment.

**Documentation Note:** These interiors are digital room mockups created for artwork visualization. They are conceptual presentations, not photographs of completed client installations.

## Documentation Standards

Each case study should include, where available:

- Project title and date.
- Type of artwork or consultation.
- Client objective or artistic challenge.
- Ela Hersberger's role and artistic decisions.
- Final artwork or documented outcome.
- Photographs or supporting references.
- Client testimonial, if permission has been obtained.

## Accuracy & Verification

- Include only verifiable professional claims.
- Distinguish completed work from planned projects.
- Clearly identify digital room mockups and conceptual studies.
- Obtain appropriate permission before publishing identifiable client information, private interiors, or testimonials.
- Preserve original testimonial wording when quoting clients.
- Do not invent client statements, project outcomes, awards, or credentials.
- Distinguish personal artistic experience from formal interior design qualifications.
- Attribute awards to the correct recipients and organizations.
