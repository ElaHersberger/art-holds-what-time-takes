# Systems & Artistic Workflows

## Art Holds What Time Takes
**Ela Hersberger — Fine Artist & Wall Art Design Specialist**

## Purpose

This directory documents the practical workflows, consultation processes, and repeatable methods used or developed by Ela Hersberger in her fine art practice.

It connects artistic philosophy with practical application in commissioned artwork, fine art curation, artwork selection, and residential interiors.

The goal is to establish clear, thoughtful processes while preserving the individuality of each client, artwork, and space.

## 1. Artistic Discovery

Understanding the individual before making artistic recommendations.

Areas of exploration include:

- Personal interests, experiences, and aspirations.
- Emotional connections to artwork.
- Existing collections and meaningful objects.
- Artistic preferences and visual influences.
- The atmosphere the client wants to experience at home.

**Guiding principle:** The person comes before the palette.

## 2. Fine Art Consultation

An emerging artist-led consultation workflow designed to connect personal identity with artwork and interiors.

Potential activities include:

- Initial client conversation.
- Discovery of personal aesthetic preferences.
- Review of the intended space.
- Exploration of artistic direction.
- Artwork recommendations.
- Review of scale, placement, and presentation.

This workflow remains in development and should not be described as a fully tested or standardized service.

## 3. Artwork Selection & Curation

A practical approach to evaluating artwork based on:

- Artistic quality and composition.
- Personal meaning and connection.
- Scale and proportion.
- Color harmony.
- Material and presentation.
- Relationship to surrounding furnishings and architecture.
- Long-term enjoyment.

Artwork may serve as the starting point for an interior or be selected to complement an existing environment.

## 4. Spatial Harmony & Art Placement

Considerations for how artwork interacts with a room:

- Wall dimensions and available space.
- Artwork size and orientation.
- Viewing distance and sightlines.
- Lighting conditions.
- Architectural features.
- Furniture relationships.
- Visual rhythm and balance.

Harmony does not require uniformity. Intentional contrast can strengthen the personality of a space.

## 5. Commissioned Fine Art

Documentation of the artistic decisions involved in creating original commissioned artwork.

Relevant areas include:

- Understanding the client's story.
- Developing the artistic concept.
- Composition and visual storytelling.
- Artwork presentation.
- Material and display considerations.
- Final selection and placement.

Specific business procedures and commercial terms belong to Masterpiece Artwork and should not automatically be presented as part of Ela Hersberger's personal methodology.

## 6. Client Presentation & Artistic Communication

Approaches to communicating artistic recommendations clearly.

Potential documentation includes:

- Artwork presentation formats.
- Written artistic explanations.
- Visual references.
- Scale and placement illustrations.
- Client feedback processes.
- Final artwork recommendations.

## 7. Professional Development

This directory may also document workflows developed through future education, collaborations, and practical experience.

Planned interior design education beginning in 2027 should be distinguished from completed training.

## Documentation Standards

Each system should identify:

- **Name:** The workflow or process being documented.
- **Purpose:** What it is designed to accomplish.
- **Status:** Established, developing, or proposed.
- **Steps:** The practical sequence of activities.
- **Application:** When and how it is used.
- **Evidence:** Completed examples, when available.
- **Limitations:** Areas requiring further testing or refinement.

Do not describe proposed systems as established professional practices without supporting evidence.

## Relationship to Artistic Frameworks

**My Process** is Ela Hersberger's established artistic framework:

Surrender · See · Honor · Reveal · Hold

**Wall Art Consultation** is an emerging five-phase methodology:

Personal Discovery · Character Interpretation · Artistic Translation · Spatial Harmony · Artwork Selection

The `frameworks` directory defines these approaches. The `systems` directory documents their practical application.
