# Research & Intellectual Foundations

## Art Holds What Time Takes
**Ela Hersberger — Fine Artist & Wall Art Design Specialist**

## Purpose

This directory documents the research, artistic influences, and intellectual foundations supporting Ela Hersberger's work in fine art, wall art design, art curation, and residential interiors.

Its purpose is to connect established knowledge with Ela's artistic philosophy and developing professional methodologies.

Research included here should support thoughtful artistic decisions without presenting unverified theories or personal interpretations as established scientific facts.

## Research Areas

### 1. Art & Human Connection

Explore how visual art relates to memory, identity, emotion, belonging, and personal experience.

Relevant disciplines include art history, aesthetics, visual culture, and environmental psychology.

### 2. Art & Residential Interiors

Study the relationship between artwork and interior spaces, including:

- Artwork scale and proportion.
- Color relationships and visual harmony.
- Lighting and artwork presentation.
- Architectural context.
- Art placement and visual balance.
- The relationship between personal identity and home environments.

### 3. Fine Art Curation

Explore approaches to selecting, arranging, and presenting artwork, including artistic coherence, collection development, material considerations, and personal preferences.

### 4. Artistic Composition

Document relevant principles of composition, color, light, visual tension, storytelling, and artistic expression.

### 5. Personal Identity & Aesthetic Preferences

Explore how individual experiences, interests, memories, and cultural influences may shape aesthetic preferences.

Research should distinguish documented findings from Ela Hersberger's personal artistic interpretations.

### 6. Professional Development

Document relevant education, reading, workshops, and research supporting Ela's expanding work in fine art and residential interiors.

Formal interior design education is planned for 2027 and should not be represented as completed.

## Research Standards

Every research document should:

- Identify its author, title, publication date, and source.
- Distinguish peer-reviewed research from professional commentary and artistic opinion.
- Explain its relevance to fine art or wall art design.
- Avoid unsupported scientific or psychological claims.
- Clearly separate external research from Ela Hersberger's original methodologies.
- Respect copyright and source attribution.

## Relationship to Artistic Frameworks

Research may inform the continued development of:

**My Process** — Ela Hersberger's established five-stage artistic framework.

**Wall Art Consultation** — Her emerging approach to connecting the individual, artwork, and interior environment.

Research findings do not automatically validate either framework.

## Documentation

Research files should use descriptive names and contain source references, a concise summary, and an explanation of their relevance.

Only include research that has been reviewed and can be traced to an identifiable source.
