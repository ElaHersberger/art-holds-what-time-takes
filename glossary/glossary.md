# Glossary

**Art Holds What Time Takes**  
**Version 2.0.0 | November 2026**

This glossary documents the established artistic philosophy, original terminology, and emerging professional concepts of Ela Hersberger.

## Foundational Artistic Philosophy

### Art Holds What Time Takes

The foundational philosophy of Ela Hersberger.

Heirloom art as emotional care. The practice of transforming love, beauty, and fleeting experiences into lasting works of art that honor personal identity, human connection, and belonging.

The philosophy now also informs Ela's approach to fine art curation, artwork placement, and the relationship between art and residential interiors.

### The Visible Love Practice

The original name associated with Ela Hersberger's five-stage artistic framework:

**Surrender. See. Honor. Reveal. Hold.**

A philosophy for recognizing love and giving it permanent artistic form.

The framework is documented in its current form as **My Process**, while its original terminology remains part of the historical record.

### Visible Love Builds Belonging

An original guiding principle.

The conviction that making love visible in the home affirms family identity and strengthens emotional connection across generations.

### Heirloom Art as Emotional Care

An original category concept connecting European elegance, classical artistic influences, human empathy, faith-led purpose, and generational significance.

It expresses the belief that meaningful artwork can provide emotional connection and a lasting sense of belonging.

### The Collector

Originally defined as discerning families and couples who value family identity, emotional connection, and classical beauty.

Defined by values rather than wealth alone, these collectors prefer fewer, deeply meaningful works of art over large collections of disposable digital images.

The expanded professional practice also considers individuals and households seeking artwork that reflects their personal identity and complements their living environments.

## Artistic Frameworks

### My Process

Ela Hersberger's established five-stage artistic framework:

1. **Surrender** — Become open to the subject and the artistic experience.
2. **See** — Recognize the meaning and character beyond the visible surface.
3. **Honor** — Respect the individuality and emotional significance of the subject.
4. **Reveal** — Express the artistic interpretation through composition, light, color, and storytelling.
5. **Hold** — Give the artistic vision lasting physical form.

### Wall Art Consultation

An emerging artist-led methodology connecting the individual, artwork, and interior environment.

Its developing five phases are Personal Discovery, Character Interpretation, Artistic Translation, Spatial Harmony, and Artwork Selection.

This methodology is not yet presented as a fully validated professional system.

## Fine Art & Interiors

### Fine Art Curation

The thoughtful selection and arrangement of artwork according to artistic quality, personal meaning, aesthetic relationships, and the environment in which the artwork will be displayed.

### Wall Art Design

An artist-led approach to selecting, composing, presenting, and positioning artwork in relation to the character of a person and the architecture of a space.

### Art for Interiors

Fine art considered in the context of residential environments, including scale, color, composition, lighting, materials, and visual relationships.

### Artwork Placement

The intentional positioning of artwork within a space, considering proportion, sightlines, furnishings, architectural features, and viewing experience.

### Spatial Harmony

The relationship between artwork and its surrounding environment.

Harmony may arise through balance, contrast, proportion, rhythm, color relationships, and intentional differences.

### Personality Before Palette

An emerging guiding principle of Ela Hersberger's consultation philosophy.

The belief that understanding the individual should precede decisions about decorative color schemes and artistic direction.

### Harmony Without Uniformity

The principle that a beautiful interior does not require every object, color, or style to match.

Intentional combinations of different artistic influences, textures, materials, and meaningful objects can create a coherent and deeply personal environment.

### The Art of Belonging

An emerging expression of the relationship between personal identity, artwork, and home.

A room should tell the story of the person who lives there.

### Connection Before Decoration

An emerging principle emphasizing personal meaning and emotional connection before purely decorative considerations.

### The Person · The Art · The Space

An emerging conceptual model describing the relationship among the individual, the selected artwork, and the interior environment.

Artwork may inspire the design of a room or be selected to complement an existing space.
