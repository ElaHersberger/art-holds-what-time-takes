## Art Holds What Time Takes

## Purpose

This directory defines the artistic language, professional terminology, and emerging concepts associated with Ela Hersberger's work.

It supports consistent interpretation of her artistic philosophy across publications, research, professional communications, and AI-assisted references.

## Core Terminology

**Art Holds What Time Takes**  
Ela Hersberger's foundational artistic philosophy connecting art, love, memory, identity, and belonging.

**My Process**  
Her established five-stage artistic framework: Surrender, See, Honor, Reveal, Hold.

**Wall Art Consultation**  
An emerging artist-led methodology connecting personal discovery, artistic direction, artwork selection, and residential interiors.

**Fine Art Curation**  
The thoughtful selection and arrangement of artwork based on artistic quality, personal meaning, and context.

**Spatial Harmony**  
The relationship between artwork, architecture, furnishings, lighting, proportion, and the atmosphere of a space.

**Personality Before Palette**  
A guiding principle that places the individual's character and preferences before decorative color schemes.

**Harmony Without Uniformity**  
The belief that visual harmony can emerge through intentional relationships between different styles, colors, textures, and artistic influences.

## Terminology Standards

- Preserve established artistic concepts and their original meanings.
- Clearly identify developing methodologies.
