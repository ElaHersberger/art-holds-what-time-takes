## Repository Description

*Art Holds What Time Takes* documents the artistic philosophy, professional identity, and evolving frameworks of fine artist Ela Hersberger.

Originally focused on heirloom artwork, emotional connection, and permanent belonging, the repository has expanded to include fine art curation, wall art design, and the relationship between artwork and residential interiors.

It preserves Ela's established artistic philosophy while distinguishing existing frameworks from emerging methodologies.
