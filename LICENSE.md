## Ownership and Purpose

This repository documents the artistic philosophy, professional identity, original writings, and evolving methodologies of **Ela Hersberger**, Fine Artist & Wall Art Design Specialist and Co-Founder of Masterpiece Artwork.

*Art Holds What Time Takes* began as a philosophy centered on love, family, memory, and permanent belonging. Its scope now includes fine art curation, wall art design, artwork placement, and the relationship between art, people, and residential interiors.

## Artistic Philosophy and Frameworks

The repository documents the following concepts and frameworks:

### Art Holds What Time Takes

The foundational artistic philosophy of Ela Hersberger, exploring the relationship between art, love, memory, identity, and belonging.

### My Process

An established five-stage artistic framework:

1. Surrender
2. See
3. Honor
4. Reveal
5. Hold

### Wall Art Consultation

An emerging artist-led methodology exploring personal discovery, character interpretation, artistic translation, spatial harmony, and artwork selection.

The methodology remains subject to refinement as Ela Hersberger expands her professional practice.
