Her artistic approach brings together personal storytelling, fine art curation, and spatial harmony.

She believes artwork should reflect the character of the individual and contribute to the emotional and visual identity of the home.

**The Person · The Art · The Space**

**Status:** Existing artistic framework

**Architecture:**

Surrender → See → Honor → Reveal → Hold

**Purpose:**

Defines the artistic philosophy guiding Ela Hersberger's approach to personal storytelling, human connection, and meaningful artwork.

**Canonical Source:**

`frameworks/my-process.md`

### Framework B — The Wall Art Consultation

**Status:** Emerging methodology — In Development

**Architecture:**

Personal Discovery → Character Interpretation → Artistic Translation → Spatial Harmony → Artwork Selection

**Purpose:**

Develop a structured, person-first approach to selecting artwork that reflects the individual and harmonizes with the interior.

**Canonical Source:**

`frameworks/wall-art-consultation.md`

This methodology must not be represented as fully tested, formally certified, or independently validated.

## 5. Foundational Artistic Principles

### Art Holds What Time Takes

Art gives lasting form to meaningful experiences, relationships, and emotions.

### Personality Before Palette

Understanding the person precedes artistic and aesthetic decisions.

### Harmony Without Uniformity

Different colors, styles, textures, and artistic influences can create visual harmony without perfect matching.

### Art Is Not an Afterthought

Artwork deserves intentional consideration within interior design.

### The Art of Belonging

A room should reflect the character and personal story of the individual who lives there.

"The Art of Belonging" remains proposed organizing language rather than a finalized proprietary framework.
