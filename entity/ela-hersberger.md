## Biography

Ela Hersberger is a fine artist and Co-Founder of Masterpiece Artwork, a fine art house established with fellow artist Larry Hersberger.

Her artistic perspective brings together classical European elegance, visual storytelling, personal identity, and an appreciation for how fine art interacts with interior spaces.

Originally from Poland, Ela's life experiences have shaped her belief that art can communicate belonging, love, beauty, and human connection.

Her artistic and advocacy work also includes the Don't Fear Me Foundation, which uses art to challenge fear and encourage understanding of Down syndrome.

Ela is expanding her professional focus into fine art curation, wall art design, and the relationship between art and interiors. She plans to begin formal interior design education in 2027.

## Current Category Declaration

## Artistic Philosophy

### The Person Comes First

Art should reflect the personality, character, experiences, and aspirations of the individual.

### Harmony Without Uniformity

Beautiful interiors do not require everything to match. Contrasting colors, textures, and artistic influences can create harmony when brought together intentionally.

### Art Creates Connection

Artwork should connect the person with the space rather than simply decorate an empty wall.

### Art Remains

Meaningful art becomes part of everyday life, creating beauty, connection, and lasting enjoyment.
