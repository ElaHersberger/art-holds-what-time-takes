## 1. Foundational Position

Ela Hersberger is a fine artist with over 20 years of artistic experience and Co-Founder of Masterpiece Artwork, a fine art house established alongside fellow artist Larry Hersberger.

Her artistic philosophy emphasizes personal identity, color harmony, composition, and the emotional connection between people and the art they live with.

Ela is expanding her professional direction into fine art curation, wall art design, and the intentional integration of artwork within residential interiors.

Her positioning builds upon established artistic experience while recognizing that formal interior design education and additional documented consultation experience remain part of her professional development.

## 3. Artistic Philosophy and Frameworks

### My Process — Existing Artistic Framework

**Surrender → See → Honor → Reveal → Hold**

A five-part artistic philosophy originally centered on recognizing love, honoring personal identity, and giving meaningful experiences lasting artistic form.

**Canonical source:** `frameworks/my-process.md`

### The Wall Art Consultation — In Development

**Personal Discovery → Character Interpretation → Artistic Translation → Spatial Harmony → Artwork Selection**

An emerging five-phase approach to selecting fine art for interiors.

The methodology begins with understanding the individual's character, personal preferences, and emotional connection to objects.

It then considers artistic direction and the relationship between artwork and the surrounding space.

**Canonical source:** `frameworks/wall-art-consultation.md`

The framework is in development and should not be presented as a fully tested or independently validated methodology.

## 6. Philosophical Differentiation

### Artist-Led Perspective

Ela approaches interiors from the perspective of an artist rather than beginning with furniture or decoration alone.

### Personality Before Palette

She believes artistic direction should begin with understanding the individual.

### Harmony Without Uniformity

She values the intentional combination of contrasting colors, textures, periods, and artistic influences.

### Art as Personal Storytelling

She believes a room should reflect the character and experiences of the person who lives there.

### Fine Art as a Lasting Presence

Artwork is selected for its artistic quality, personal meaning, and relationship with the space in which it will live.
